Our network

Authors and partners who show up for young writers.

Published authors lead our sessions, and our sponsors keep every program free.

Partner & program authors

Sara Furlong

Children's book author and storyteller with more than 20 books, including the award-winning Every Brain is Beautiful series.

Jennifer Maruno

Author of eight novels and nine picture books for young readers, recognised on Bank Street's 2023 Best Children's Books list and with 2025 Stepping Stones and Horace Mann honours.

Matteo Cerilli

Transmasc author and activist writing speculative fiction for all ages, including the YA horror novel Lockjaw and the middle-grade ghost story Something's Up with Arlo.

Susan Currie

Haudenosaunee-Acadian writer for young people, with middle-grade, YA, and nonfiction books including the White Pine nominee Iz the Apocalypse.

Hannah Manders

Indie fantasy and sci-fi author in southern Ontario with five projects in progress, including Death and Rest.

Shelly Sanders

Bestselling historical fiction author writing about unsung women, including The Night Sparrow and Daughters of the Occupation.

Free program series and community events, run with partners who care about young voices.

Program series · 6 weeks

NEVERSAID × Arthouse Halton

A six-week program of creative writing coaching sessions with the youth at Arthouse Halton. Authors from a range of genres were invited to share their advice with aspiring young writers.

Community event

NEVERSAID @ Salvation Army Literary Salon

A literary discussion at a Salvation Army emergency shelter, featuring best-selling author and journalist Shelly Sanders (HarperCollins) and local dark fantasy author Hannah Manders.

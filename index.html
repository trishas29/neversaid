What we do

Free programs, led by students and taught alongside established authors, for anyone with something to say.

01

Free sessions

Workshops and coaching with published authors from across the industry, at no cost to young writers.

02

A writers' network

Every session brings in new local authors, so the community keeps growing and everyone finds their own path.

03

Words that last

We help writers turn their own experiences into memories on the page that they can keep and come back to.

Our mission

Creative writing belongs to everyone.

The Neversaid Society is a student-led non-profit in Oakville, Ontario. We make creative writing accessible to all and build a community of writers who are free from conformity.

Free programs & sessions

We partner with established authors across the industry to offer writing programs and sessions that are completely free.

A growing writers' network

Every time we invite local authors to a session, our network grows. Members support each other's individual paths instead of fitting into one mould.

Growing up together

We celebrate honest, unfinished work that reflects life in progress, written for and read by people of every age.

Preserving memory with words

Through wordplay, our sessions help writers turn their own experiences into memories they can keep and revisit.
